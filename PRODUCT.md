# Product

## Register

product

## Users

U.S. multi-card holders at the payment moment (online checkout or in-store). They need an answer in seconds, often on a phone, without researching category rules.

## Product Purpose

Recommend the best card in the user's wallet for the current merchant and show why. Success is a trusted, fast recommendation the user can act on immediately.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, trustworthy. Like a payment utility, not a marketing site. Tool-first; numbers and card names do the talking.

## Anti-references

Dark fintech glassmorphism, neon green accents, gradient hero cards, decorative blur, landing-page typography, "AI startup" dashboards.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed to answer** — the recommended card is visible with minimal scrolling.
2. **Earned familiarity** — patterns users know from wallet and banking apps.
3. **Restrained color** — neutral gray structure; teal only for actions and key numbers.
4. **No decoration without function** — flat surfaces, readable type, obvious controls.
5. **Accessible contrast** — WCAG AA on all body and label text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets ≥44px where practical.
