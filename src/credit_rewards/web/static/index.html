    How we estimate $

    We show one estimated dollar value so you can compare cards at checkout.

    Points cards: points earned × official cents-per-point (CPP). Example: 400 pts × 2.2¢ ≈ $8.80.

    At a partner store (e.g. delta.com), we value that brand's points at typical in-brand redemption rates—so your co-brand card's earn shows fairly in $.

    Cash-back cards: purchase × earn rate. Example: $100 × 6% = $6.00 cash back.

    Not guaranteed cash. Redemption value varies by how you use rewards.
